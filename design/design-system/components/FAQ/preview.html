What does the watermark look like?

A small "Pulsefield" in the bottom right corner. The editor's preview shows it exactly where the video will have it.

Do I keep my projects on the free plan?

Yes. Projects, your own templates and your videos stay in your account on both plans.

How is the payment handled?

By Stripe, on a page run by Stripe. Card details never reach this site.

What happens when Premium ends?

The account goes back to the free plan. Nothing is deleted.
